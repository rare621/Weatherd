Annoyingly, it's also a really good weather app

Hourly and 16-day forecasts

48-hour charts for temperature, rain, wind and UV, an hour-by-hour list, and 16 days ahead. Tap any hour for every detail.

Radar that runs ahead

Two hours of live radar, then up to 12 hours of forecast precipitation, with smooth transitions and automatic rain, snow and storm colours.

Work-site checks

A go / caution / stop verdict for the next six hours: heat, cold, ice, wind, lightning, rain, visibility, UV and air quality.

Good working windows

When conditions suit concrete, paint and coatings, roofing, and cranes and lifts over the next 24 hours.

Next precipitation

Minute-by-minute rain timing for the next four hours, so you know if you have 20 minutes or 2.

Severe weather alerts

National Weather Service watches and warnings for the US, like tornado watches and wind advisories.

Home screen widgets

Small, hourly, and hourly plus 5-day widgets that refresh on their own, so your home screen can insult you too.

Animated sky

Clouds, rain, snow, sleet, hail, lightning bolts, stars and wind that match the real weather outside.

Questions people ask

Is there really a weather app that swears at you?

Yes, this one. It's free on Android and it roasts you about the forecast. Turn it off in Settings → Potty mouth if your mom borrows your phone.

What's the best weather app for construction workers?

We're biased, but Weathered was built for exactly that: six-hour go / caution / stop checks, lightning and wind warnings for lifts and ladders, and good windows for concrete, paint and roofing.

Is it free?

Completely. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no subscription. It's open source under GPL-3.0.

How do I install it?

Download Weathered.apk from the releases page and open it on your Android phone. Your phone will warn you about installing apps from outside the Play Store. It's being dramatic.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. Android only, for now.
